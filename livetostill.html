Live to Still – Support

Free iPhone storage cleanup app by Trader Goods

What it does

  • Convert Live Photos to stills at the original quality. The original photo data is kept, with no re-encoding. The video part is deleted by default, or you can turn on Keep video clips to save each clip as a separate video.
  • Works when storage is full. Conversion runs in small chunks and pauses if space runs out.
  • Empty Recently Deleted shows the steps to free up space for good in the Photos app.
  • Delete Copies finds an original video and an edited copy of it (for example, one with text or stickers added) from the same day. Choose Keep Original or Keep Edited.

FAQ

Why is Clips empty? Clips only fill up if Keep video clips is on before you convert.

Why didn't my storage go down? Deleted items stay in Recently Deleted for up to 30 days. Empty that album in the Photos app.
