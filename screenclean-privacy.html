Privacy Policy

Screen Clean · Published by Trader Goods · Effective November 18, 2026

This policy covers the iPhone and iPad app Screen Clean.

No data collected

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No ads, analytics, or tracking
  • No in-app purchases
  • No network connections: the app works entirely on your device

Brightness

While the cleaning screen is on, the app lowers the screen brightness so smudges are easier to see, then puts it back. That change stays on your device. Nothing is saved or sent.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions: support@tradergoods.us.
