Privacy Policy

The Hormuz Strait · Published by Trader Goods · Effective October 14, 2026

This policy explains what information the game The Hormuz Strait uses. It applies to the browser version and the iOS app.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No ads
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No purchases with real money
  • No contact, location, photos, or other personal data from your device

Data stored on your device

Game progress such as oil, unlocks, and your best score is saved only on your device (for example in the browser’s local storage). Clearing the app or site data removes it. We cannot see or restore that data.

Optional global leaderboard

If you choose to submit a score, we store:

  • The display name you enter (letters and numbers only, up to 16 characters)
  • Your score

That name and score are shown publicly on the leaderboard, hosted with Supabase. Please do not use your real name or other identifying information.

When a score is submitted, the server may briefly record the IP address solely to limit spam and abuse. IP addresses are not shown on the leaderboard and are not used for advertising or profiling.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience and is not directed at collecting personal information from children. Do not enter a child’s real name or personal details on the leaderboard.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/Trader-Goods/hormuz-strait/issues.

We may update this page if the game’s data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when that happens.
