Privacy Policy

Live to Still · Published by Trader Goods · Effective October 19, 2026

This policy covers the iPhone app Live to Still.

No data collected

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No ads, analytics, or tracking
  • No in-app purchases
  • No network connections: the app works entirely on your device

Photo library access

The app asks for access to your photo library so it can find Live Photos and duplicate videos, convert Live Photos to still photos, and delete the items you choose. All of this happens on your iPhone. Your photos and videos are never uploaded, copied off the device, or shared with us or anyone else.

Deletions go through the standard iOS confirmation, and deleted items move to the Photos app's Recently Deleted album.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/Trader-Goods/hormuz-strait/issues.

If the app's data practices change, this page and its effective date will be updated.
