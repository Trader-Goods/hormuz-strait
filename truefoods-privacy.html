Privacy Policy

TrueFoods · Published by Trader Goods · Effective November 16, 2026

This policy covers the iPhone app TrueFoods.

No account

  • No sign-in and no profile
  • No ads and no tracking
  • No in-app purchases
  • Favorites, recent scans, and ingredient flags stay on your iPhone

Camera and photos

The camera is used to read a barcode or a label. You can also pick a label photo from your library. The picture is read on your iPhone. TrueFoods does not upload your photos.

Lookups

When you search or scan, the app sends the food name or barcode to Open Food Facts to load the product. If a nutrition gap remains, it may send the food name to USDA FoodData Central. A recall check may send the product name or barcode to openFDA. These requests are not tied to an account. TrueFoods does not sell this information.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions: support@tradergoods.us.

If the app's data practices change, this page and its effective date will be updated.
