Screen Clean – Support

Free iPhone and iPad app by Trader Goods

What it does

  • One press turns the glass black for one minute so fingerprints and dust are easier to see.
  • Wipes do not tap anything. The phone stays awake. Hold the screen to stop early.
  • Brightness comes back to where you left it when the minute ends.

How to wipe

Use a clean, soft cloth. Tilt the phone under a lamp. Do not spray liquid on the glass. This app does not wash the screen for you.
