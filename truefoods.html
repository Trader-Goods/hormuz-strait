TrueFoods – Support

Free iPhone app by Trader Goods

What it does

  • Scan a barcode or search for a packaged food sold in the United States.
  • A score from 0 to 100 starts at 100 and goes down for dyes, additives, sugar, salt, and processing. The card explains the reasons in plain words.
  • Your flags mark ingredients you want to avoid. A flagged ingredient turns the card red.
  • Favorites and recent scans stay on your iPhone.

FAQ

Why is a card gray with “- -”? The ingredient list has not loaded, or the product has no ingredient list. TrueFoods does not invent a score in that case.

Is this medical advice? No. It is a guide to the label. Check the package if you have an allergy.

Why did a search miss a product? Search covers products in the United States that Open Food Facts knows about. A barcode scan is the more direct lookup.
